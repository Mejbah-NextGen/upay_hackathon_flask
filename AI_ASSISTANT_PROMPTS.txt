UpayX App Assistant — sample prompts
====================================
Open the star button beside Notifications, or visit App Assistant.
Copy a prompt below. English, বাংলা and romanized Bangla are supported.
Replies use your signed-in account. Payment buttons open a form for you to
review and confirm; the assistant does not execute payments. All funds and
providers in this app are demonstrations. Use Clear chat to start a new topic.

FOLLOW-UP EXAMPLES
1. Ask: How do I pay university fees?
   Then ask: And the fee for BDT 500?
2. Ask: What is the agent cash out fee for BDT 500?
   Then ask: What about ATM for BDT 1,000?
3. Ask: What did I spend this month?
   Then ask: And today?

For fee questions, name the service and amount. Your institution supplies
its actual tuition/admission/examination amount; the app does not look it up.
Only the entered education/bill amount is deducted in this demo, with no
additional service charge. Cash Out has separate agent/ATM charges.
