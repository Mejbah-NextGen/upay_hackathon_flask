Planning research pilot

Help us test financial planning and recurring payment management with observed task outcomes.

Financial Health

What we are testing

Does a cash-flow forecast alongside the recorded commitment estimate help people complete a planning review? Participants receive either the existing commitment estimate (control) or the estimate plus the forecast (treatment). Auto Pay and the planning acknowledgement are available to both groups.

This is a wallet prototype. Every payment is simulated, including payments made by real research participants. The study does not establish real revenue or financial improvement.
