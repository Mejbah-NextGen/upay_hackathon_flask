Model and evaluation evidence

A supervised model is trained offline to predict seven-day everyday wallet outflow. Its evaluation compares held-out predictions with observed synthetic outcomes and simple spending-average baselines. The trained parameters run locally without an AI API.

The current evaluation demonstrates the implementation on synthetic histories. Real customer forecasting accuracy and customer impact have not yet been validated.
