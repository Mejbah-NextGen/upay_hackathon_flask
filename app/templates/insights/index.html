How this estimate works
  1. Reserve unpaid schedules due by the end of this seven-day window, including overdue and failed items. Both automatic and manual plans count. Completed, cancelled and successfully linked payments are excluded.
  2. Reserve outstanding Pay Later purchases due in this window or earlier. Repaid purchases and successfully linked repayments are excluded. Later obligations remain in Pay Later.
  3. Supported schedules are Send Money, Mobile Recharge and Bill Payment. Their current demo fee is zero. Pay Later repayment also has zero demo fee.
  4. Safe-to-spend is the wallet balance minus those reserves, with a minimum of zero. A negative remainder appears as a funding gap. Saved savings calculators do not reserve or debit money.
  5. Unusual-payment comparisons require at least five earlier successful payments of the same service type within 30 days. Failed, pending and deferred ledger entries do not affect observed cash flow or the reconciliation.
